\section{Introduction}
In this note, we extend some of the results from synthetic Stone duality (SSD, \cite{synthetic-stone-duality}) to talk about uniform continuity of functions between compact Hausdorff spaces. We will then define the topologists sine curve as a compact Hausdorff space in the setting of SSD. We will show that the topologists sine curve is not path-connect, but it is $[0,1]$-contractible. We will recall that any path-connected space is $[0,1]$-contractible, but thus conclude the converse cannot hold.
